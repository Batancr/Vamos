          How you may travel

          All times are estimates from Vamos's simple travel model: average speeds including sleep, a 28 km map grid, and roads and rails assumed wherever there's land (short bridges and tunnels included). Flights use real airport locations from OurAirports (airports with scheduled service), but the times are worked out, not taken from timetables: 3 hours at the airports, 30 minutes of taxiing and 800 km/h in the air. It doesn't check whether an airline really flies that route; big airports may fly up to 15,000 km and smaller ones up to 2,500 km. Countries and cities from Natural Earth.

          Planes are banned in the game. Terrain from AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen; SRTM, GMTED, ETOPO1 and others). Borders from Natural Earth via world-atlas; lakes from Natural Earth. Streets from © OpenStreetMap contributors. Port locations from Natural Earth. Characters are original, inspired by well-known stories.
